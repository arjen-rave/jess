How it works

A little something for every day of your year. Here's how it works.

For Jess 🎂
📅
Today
Each day you get one new card — a little something just for you. Open the app, read it, carry on.
📆
Calendar
Tap the calendar tab to browse your past cards. Tap any highlighted date to see what card you got that day. Future dates are locked — no peeking.
🔥
Streak
The flame shows how many days in a row you've opened the app. No pressure. But also, a little pressure.
⏰
Daily reminder
Set the time you want a nudge to open the app in Settings. Works once installed on your iPhone.
🔀
Reshuffle
If you want to mix things up, you can reshuffle your future cards in Settings. Your past cards stay exactly as they were.
⚙️
Dev Mode
Unlocks advanced controls — access via Settings. Password protected. Dev mode lets you view the full card schedule, browse the complete deck, change the start date, add custom special dates, and perform a full reset. Not for everyday use.

Reshuffle future cards?

Full reset?

Clears everything — seen cards, shuffle order, streak — and reshuffles from today. Nuclear option.

Change start date?

This will reset the schedule and reshuffle from the new start date.

Before we set up your daily reminder

To send you notifications when the app is closed, we need to store a notification token on a small private server. This token is not personal data — it's a random code that lets us reach your phone. It's stored securely, used only to send your daily card reminder, and deleted the moment you turn notifications off.

This server is run by Arjen, not a third party.
