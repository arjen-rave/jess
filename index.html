How it works
For Jess 🎂
📅
Today
Each day you get one new card — a little something just for you. Open the app, read it, carry on.
📆
Calendar
Tap the calendar tab to browse your past cards. Tap any highlighted date to see what card you got that day. Future dates are locked — no peeking.
🔥
Streak
The flame shows how many days in a row you've opened the app. No pressure. But also, a little pressure.
⏰
Daily reminder
Set the time you want a nudge to open the app in Settings. Works once installed on your iPhone.
🔀
Reshuffle
If you want to mix things up, you can reshuffle your future cards in Settings. Your past cards stay exactly as they were.
For contributors ✍️
🃏
What is this?
This is an alpha version of a birthday app being built for Jess. Each card is an affirmation — funny, sarcastic, or surprisingly real. Jess gets one per day.
✍️
How to contribute
Write a quote, pick a vibe and a look. That's it. Submit one at a time, but as many as you like — the goal is 365+.
💡
What makes a good card?
Something that sounds like Jess, or sounds like something someone would say to Jess. Sarcastic, self-aware, funny, caring, upbeat. Bonus points if it's embarrassingly relatable.
🏷️
Tags
Some cards fit a specific moment — Monday dread, Friday relief, Halloween chaos. If yours does, tag it. Otherwise "any day" is fine.
🎨
Design
Help finalise the design by suggesting an icon (or upload a pic) and a background colour in the form. I'll collate and create a full list of cards as they come in.
